Language apps for learning two languages at once

Duolingo, Babbel, Busuu and LingQ each let you study a second language next to your first. Polingual teaches your languages in one set with one review. Every fact about another app links to a page that company published.

Polingual and Duolingo for two languages at once

Duolingo lets you add a second course next to your first and keeps one streak across them. Polingual puts your languages in one set and teaches each meaning in all of them together.

Polingual and Babbel for two languages at once

Babbel teaches 14 languages through short lessons written by its own language experts, and its all-languages plans let you move between them. Polingual puts your languages in one set and reviews them together.

Polingual and Busuu for two languages at once

Busuu offers 14 language courses and a community of fluent speakers who give feedback on your speaking and writing. Polingual puts your languages in one set and reviews them together.

Polingual and LingQ for two languages at once

LingQ teaches through reading and listening to real content in 53 languages, and Premium members have access to all of them. Polingual puts your languages in one set and reviews their words together.
