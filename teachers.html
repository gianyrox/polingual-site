Polingual for language teachers

A live word quiz for the whole class. You host on the projector and students answer on their phones. One game takes about ten minutes.

Start a class gameDownload on the App Store

Ten minutes in class

  1. Open the board

    Open polingual.com/live on the classroom projector and sign in with your email. Choose the language for the prompts and up to 12 languages to quiz.

  2. Students join

    The board shows a 6 character room code and a QR code. Students scan the QR code or open polingual.com/join and enter the room code. Each student picks a nickname from the names offered.

  3. Play the game

    The game runs 3 rounds of 5 questions, with 20 seconds for each. Every question has four answers, and students tap one on their phones.

  4. Read the board

    The board shows standings after each round and a final board at the end. Download the results file to keep the scores by nickname.

Questions

Most questions ask the class to pick a word or a translation in one of your quiz languages. Others show a word and ask which language it belongs to. A class that studies Spanish and French gets both in the same game.

Classroom controls

School mode

Removes app links and promotions from the board and from every phone.

Teams

Chorus mode splits the class into 2 to 4 teams. Open Floor has every student play alone.

Lock and remove

Lock the room once your class is in. Remove any player from the lobby.

Pause and Next

Pause the game to talk about an answer, or press Next to move on.

Questions from teachers

What does it cost?

Hosting and joining are free.

Do students need accounts?

No. Students join with the room code and a nickname picked from a short list. They give no email and no real name. The teacher signs in with an email to host.

Which languages does the quiz cover?

The quiz covers 22 languages: Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, German, Dutch, Swedish, Russian, Polish, Greek, Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Hindi, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Turkish, Vietnamese, English, and Latin. Prompts can appear in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Arabic, Hebrew, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hindi, Russian, and Turkish.

How many students can join?

One room holds up to 100 players.

What do students need?

A phone or another device with a web browser. The game runs on a web page, with no install.

What happens to student data?

Players give no personal details, and room records are deleted 24 hours after the room closes. The student data page at polingual.com/live/student-data has the details.
