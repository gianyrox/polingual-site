01 · Story

Learn a family of languages at once

Polingual teaches a word once and shows it in every sibling language, with its sound and its roots. A study app and a party game, from the team behind Bucket Foundation.

After 100 days, expect to know 280 words across 4 languages, and remember 95% of them.

FSRS simulation, 5 new words a day per language, 8 runs. polingual/scripts/sim_100_days.ts

  • 28languages
  • 1,000top words each
  • 300phrases each
  • 12party games

Who it is for

Heritage speakers, travelers, families with two or more home languages and anyone who wants more than one language at a time. Proceeds fund Bucket Foundation, which keeps primary research free to read.

07 · Illustration

Illustration rules

Every screen is part of one illustrated world built from the homepage hero.

  • DOUse the hero artwork as it is drawn: thick ink outlines, flat fills, one hard shadow.
  • DOTilt tiles and stickers a few degrees and let them overlap on tyrian or paper.
  • DOPair every script with its own Noto face so the word reads right.
  • DOScatter confetti dots in white and vermilion at the edges of a scene.
  • DON'TGeneric icon packs, emoji or stock photos in place of the artwork.
  • DON'TGradients, glows or soft blurred shadows on the art.
  • DON'TRedrawn flags or invented scripts.
  • DON'TArt packed in a rigid grid on white with no tilt.
08 · Motion

Motion

Three durations and one springy ease. Motion confirms a tap and then gets out of the way.

Press · 100 msButtons sink onto their lip.--pl-motion-press
Pop · 220 msTiles, stickers and badges arrive.--pl-motion-pop
Settle · 420 msCards land and rank rows slide.--pl-motion-settle

Ease cubic-bezier(.2,.9,.3,1.2), token --pl-motion-ease. Reduced motion turns every demo off.
